Our parish bears the name of Abune Tekle Haymanot, one of the saints of the Ethiopian Church.

He was born in the 13th century at Zorare in Selale, in the region of Shewa. Tradition dates his birth to around 1215. According to tradition, his father was Tsega Ze'ab, a priest, and his mother was Egzi'e Haraya.

In Shewa he founded the monastery of Debre Asbo. From the 15th century onward, the monastery he founded has been known as Debre Libanos. He died around 1313.

According to his hagiography, he stood in prayer for so long that one of his legs broke, and he went on standing on one foot for seven years. The hagiography also tells that God gave him six wings.

The Church commemorates Abune Tekle Haymanot on the 24th day of every Ethiopian month. Among his annual feasts are his birth, celebrated on Tahsas 24, and his repose, celebrated on Nehase 24.
