From the parish's program (Nehase 22–23, 2018 E.C.):

On Friday, August 28, 2026, the tabot of our father Abune Teklehaymanot
was received with hymns and escorted with thanksgiving to the church,
arriving at 1:30 PM. On Saturday, August 29, the Mahlet (praise) service
began at 3:00 AM and continued until dawn, followed by the morning Divine
Liturgy; the feast program then continued with the Sunday school choirs'
hymns, teaching, and the blessing of the fathers.

The program was held with the blessing of His Grace Abune Yaekob and the
honored fathers. The program poster is in our Gallery.
