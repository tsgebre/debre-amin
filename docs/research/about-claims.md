# About page — claims register

Research by the RESEARCHER specialist (P1-09). Every claim that appears in
`src/content/about/*.md` or the Services prose (`src/i18n/*.json`,
`services.*`) is listed here with its source. Status is one of `verified`,
`verified-as-tradition` (hagiographic material, framed as tradition in the
prose) or `dropped` (not shipped). "Shipped" says whether the claim appears
in site prose.

Access notes: Britannica returned 403 and could not be consulted. Wikipedia
`Tekle_Haymanot` was seen only as a search snippet, so it is not cited as a
source for any shipped claim.
